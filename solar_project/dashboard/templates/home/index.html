What is State of Charge?

State of Charge (SOC) is the percentage of energy remaining in your battery — like a fuel gauge. 100% is fully charged. Most systems avoid dropping below 20% to protect battery lifespan.

Battery SOC Range Guide

What your battery level means for your home's energy independence.

80% – 100%
Fully or near-fully charged. Your system is storing surplus solar energy. You are operating at maximum energy independence.
50% – 79%
Good charge level. Your battery can cover several hours of typical home load without any solar input or grid assistance.
20% – 49%
Moderate reserve. Your system may begin drawing from the grid depending on your settings. Expect replenishment once the sun rises.
Below 20%
Low reserve. Most battery systems set this as the minimum discharge limit to protect long-term battery health. Grid import is likely active.
